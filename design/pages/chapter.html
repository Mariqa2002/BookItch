Chapter 3

Make It Obvious

The easier you make a habit to notice, the more likely you are to do it.

Key Takeaways

Design your environment to cue the habit.

Use visual reminders (e.g. post-its, alarms, triggers).

Make the desired behaviour obvious, not the undesired one.
